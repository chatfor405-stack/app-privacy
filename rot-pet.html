Privacy Policy

1Data we collect

We do not collect your personal data, and we run no servers.

The only time Rot-Pet uses the Internet is to check and process Premium purchases, through the App Store or Google Play and our payment provider RevenueCat (see section 4). Your usage stats, chosen apps, check-ins and everything else in section 2 are never sent anywhere.

Rot-Pet is a screen-time app for Android: a virtual brain gets healthier when you use your phone better, and the app asks you to pause before opening apps you chose to limit. All data it uses is created and kept only on your device, in the app's private storage.

2Data stored on your device

To work, Rot-Pet keeps this information locally:

Apps you pick to limit, your daily budget, your brain's nameYour settings
Minutes spent per app per day (from Android Usage Access)Rot Score, stats, weekly report and "before vs now" proof
Check-in events (shown, left or unlocked, challenge results)Game progress: rot level, brain cells, streak
List of installed apps (name and icon)So you can pick which apps to limit
In-app activity counts (e.g. "report shared")The optional beta stats summary (section 5)

This data never leaves your phone unless you share something yourself.

3Permissions and how they are used

Screen Time (iOS 16 and later)

On iOS, you authorize Screen Time and choose individual apps in Apple's private picker. Rot-Pet stores opaque app tokens and blocking settings in a private App Group shared with its Device Activity monitor extension. Managed Settings applies Apple's shield immediately or when the selected apps reach a shared daily limit. Tokens and usage thresholds are not sent to RevenueCat. You can turn blocking off or revoke authorization in Settings. Android accessibility, usage-statistics and challenge features below do not apply to this iOS flow.

Accessibility Service ("Rot-Pet App Check-ins")

Used only to detect which app is opened, so Rot-Pet can show a check-in screen when you open an app you chose to limit.

  • It listens only for "window changed" events, to read the package name of the app in front.
  • It is configured so it cannot read screen content. It does not read, collect or store your messages, passwords, what you type, or anything shown inside other apps.
  • It does not change, click or control anything in other apps.
  • Nothing it observes is sent anywhere.

You turn this service on yourself in Android settings, and you can turn it off at any time. The app explains this clearly before asking.

Usage Access

Used to read how many minutes you spent in each app, for your stats, Rot Score and weekly report. Processed and stored on your phone only.

Notifications

For the optional daily 9 PM verdict, and alerts when blocking stops working. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

Run at startup

Restores your daily reminder and the blocking health check after the phone restarts.

Internet and store billing

Used only for Premium: to show prices, buy, restore, and check whether your Premium is active. See section 4.

List of installed apps

Rot-Pet asks Android for apps that appear in your launcher, so you can choose which ones to limit. The list is shown on your phone and not sent anywhere.

4Premium purchases and third parties

Rot-Pet does not use advertising, analytics or crash reporting services, and it never sells your data.

Premium is optional. If you open the Premium screen, buy, or restore a purchase, two services are involved:

  • Apple's App Store or Google Play handles the payment. Your card and billing details go to the store, never to us. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat (our payment provider) checks the purchase and remembers that this app copy owns Premium. It receives: a random anonymous ID created by the app (not your name, email or store account), purchase details (which plan, price, currency, purchase and renewal dates, and store transaction information), and basic technical information sent with every Internet request, such as device model, operating system and app version, country or language, and IP address. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

None of your Rot-Pet data (stats, apps, check-ins, brain) is included. We use the purchase information only to unlock Premium, restore it on a new phone, and keep basic sales records. Manage subscriptions in Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, depending on where you bought them.

5What you can share yourself

Some features let you share content by your own choice, through Android's standard share sheet (for example to WhatsApp or email):

  • Weekly report card: an image of your weekly results.
  • Beta stats: a short text with totals only. It contains no app names and no personal details, and you can read it before sharing.

Once you share something, the app you shared it with handles it under its own policy.

Ratings and reviews

Now and then Rot-Pet may show Google Play's built-in review card, and Settings has a link to the app's Play Store page. Ratings and reviews you leave are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. Rot-Pet itself never sees or receives them.

6Data retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your phone as long as the app is installed. Old activity records are trimmed automatically.
  • To delete everything, uninstall Rot-Pet, or clear its data: Settings → Apps → Rot-Pet → Storage → Clear data.
  • We hold no copy of your app data, so there is nothing of it to delete on a server.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as long as needed for billing, refunds and legal requirements. To ask for your purchase data to be deleted, contact us (section 10) and we will request it from RevenueCat.

7Children

Rot-Pet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read under Android's standard security model. It never leaves your phone. Purchase checks with Google Play and RevenueCat use encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

9Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts sending any other data off your phone, this policy will be updated first and the app will tell you clearly before it happens.
