Privacy Policy

OfflineScribe · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Short version: OfflineScribe does not collect, store or share any of your data. Your recordings and transcripts never leave your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your device

Audio recordings, imported files, transcripts, bookmarks, folders and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone using the open-source whisper.cpp engine and OpenAI's Whisper models. Recordings and transcripts are excluded from Google cloud backup, so they are not copied off your phone by the app or by Android backup.

Files you import

When you import audio or video (with the in-app picker or by sharing a file to OfflineScribe), the app reads only the file you chose and stores its own copy in its private storage. It does not browse or read any other files on your phone.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only to:

  • download speech-recognition models from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) when you tap GET or UPDATE on a model. Models are between about 40 MB and 830 MB; by default they download over Wi-Fi only;
  • process an optional one-time "Pro" purchase, and restore it, through Google Play Billing.

No audio, transcript or personal information is sent in these requests. As with any download, the servers involved (Hugging Face, Google Play) can see your device's IP address; see their privacy policies for how they handle it. Recording and transcribing work fully offline.

No tracking

OfflineScribe contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and has no account system or backend server. We have no way to see what you record or transcribe.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The app receives only a purchase token from Google Play to confirm that Pro is unlocked; we never receive your name, email or payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payments.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record audio, only while you are recording. A notification is shown the whole time.
  • Notifications: to show recording, transcription and download progress.
  • Internet and network state: only for the model downloads and purchases described above.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: so long recordings and transcriptions keep running while you use other apps or the screen is off.

Feedback emails

"Send feedback" in Settings opens a draft in your own email app with your phone model and Android version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and you can edit or remove anything in it first.

Recording other people

Laws about recording conversations differ by place (for example, some US states require everyone's consent). You are responsible for getting consent where required.

Deleting your data

Delete any recording or model in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is no data on any server to request or delete.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
