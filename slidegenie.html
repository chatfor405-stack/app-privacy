SlideGenie Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

SlideGenie is a presentation-generation and editing application. This policy explains what information the service processes and why.

Information we process

Account information: email address and optional profile name used for authentication and account management.

Presentation data: presentation titles, slide content, formatting, layouts, and templates that you create or edit.

Uploaded images: images you choose to upload to your presentations.

AI prompts and generated content: prompts sent to the SlideGenie generation service and the presentation content returned by the AI provider.

License information: AppSumo entitlement status and a securely hashed license code used to validate lifetime activation.

Usage information: AI generation usage needed to enforce the free and activated monthly limits.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, save and restore presentations, generate presentations, enforce usage limits, process AppSumo activation, provide exports, maintain security, and respond to support requests.

Third-party services

SlideGenie uses infrastructure and service providers including Supabase for authentication, database and storage, and Google Gemini for presentation generation. Information sent to a third-party service is limited to what is necessary for the requested feature.

On Android, Google Analytics for Firebase records app opens and may process app and device information and app-instance identifiers to measure usage. See Google's Privacy Policy.

For optional subscriptions, the app store processes payment. RevenueCat uses the app account identifier and purchase and entitlement information to manage and restore subscription access. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

AppSumo activation

License codes are validated server-side. SlideGenie stores a cryptographic hash of an imported AppSumo code rather than the plaintext code.

Data retention and deletion

Your account and associated application data are retained while your account is active. You can initiate deletion from Settings → Delete account or contact us with a deletion request. Account deletion is intended to remove the associated application data, subject to backups, security logs, and legal obligations that may require limited retention.

Security

We use authenticated requests, row-level access controls where supported, server-side license validation, and server-side AI usage enforcement. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

SlideGenie is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
