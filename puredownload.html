PureDownload Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

PureDownload ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a media downloading application for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use PureDownload.

1. Information We Collect

PureDownload does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide your name, phone number, password, or other personal account information.

The App may process information that you voluntarily provide through the App, including media or webpage URLs that you enter or share with PureDownload for the purpose of resolving and downloading media.

Download information and media-related metadata may be processed locally on your device to provide features such as download management, download history, media previews, file names, download status, quality information, and download progress.

2. URLs and Media Information

When you provide a URL to PureDownload, the App may send that URL to the relevant third-party website or service in order to retrieve publicly available media information and available media streams.

Depending on the URL you provide, the request may be processed by the website or platform associated with that URL. These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

PureDownload does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or platforms.

3. Local Storage

PureDownload stores download-related information locally on your Android device in order to provide features such as download history, download progress, completed-download information, and download management.

Downloaded media files are stored on your device in the location provided by the App, such as the PureDownload folder.

This locally stored information is not intentionally uploaded by PureDownload to a personal account or a PureDownload server.

4. Network Access

PureDownload requires Internet access to resolve media information and download media from URLs provided by the user.

Network requests may contain the URL supplied by the user and may be sent to the corresponding third-party website or service.

5. Permissions

PureDownload may request Android permissions necessary for its functionality.

  • Internet: Used to retrieve media information and download media, request advertisements and, if you allow usage analytics, send analytics events.
  • Network State: Used to determine network connectivity.
  • Notifications: Used to show download progress and completion notifications.
  • Storage: On supported older Android versions, storage permission may be required to save downloaded files.
  • Foreground Service: Used to support downloads that continue while the application is not actively displayed.
  • Advertising ID: Google Mobile Ads may use the Android advertising ID to provide and measure advertising and prevent fraud, subject to your consent and device settings. You can reset or delete this identifier in Android settings.

6. Data Sharing

PureDownload does not rent or trade your download history or downloaded files. Google advertising services process the data described below; applicable privacy choices are available in the App.

PureDownload does not operate a user account system or a personal cloud-storage service.

However, when you provide a media URL, the URL may be transmitted to the third-party website or service associated with that URL as necessary for the App to retrieve media information or media streams.

Those third-party services operate independently and may collect or process information according to their own policies.

7. Analytics and Advertising

PureDownload uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner advertisements. The advertising SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can be used to estimate approximate location), device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic and performance information. Google uses this information to provide and measure advertisements, perform analytics, and help prevent fraud and abuse. Advertising data is encrypted in transit.

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before advertisements can be requested. You can revisit these choices under Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices when this option applies to your region. Advertisement availability depends on connectivity, consent status and Google's ad-serving systems.

Usage analytics is optional. Where the consent platform reports that consent is not required, it is enabled by default unless you have disabled it. Otherwise, it stays disabled until you enable it in Settings. If enabled, Google Firebase Analytics collects app usage events, screen views, app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and engagement information to help us understand and improve the App. Google may use network information to derive approximate location. We do not send your download URLs, media titles, file names, download history or downloaded files to Firebase Analytics. We disable advertising personalisation signals and advertising consent categories for Firebase Analytics.

You can allow or disable usage analytics at any time under Settings > Privacy > Usage analytics. Disabling it stops future Firebase Analytics collection; it does not automatically remove data already processed by Google. Contact us if you have a data privacy request.

For details about Google's processing and available controls, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

8. Children's Privacy

PureDownload is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through PureDownload, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

PureDownload may interact with third-party websites and platforms based on URLs supplied by the user. These services may have their own privacy policies, terms of service, and data practices.

Users should review the privacy policies and terms of the relevant third-party services when using their content or services.

11. Copyright and User Responsibility

PureDownload is a technical tool for downloading media from URLs provided by the user.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of downloaded content complies with applicable laws, copyright requirements, platform rules, and the rights of content owners.

PureDownload does not claim ownership of content downloaded by users.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable requirements, or our practices.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.
