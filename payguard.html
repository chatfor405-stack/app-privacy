PayGuard Privacy Policy

Your records stay on your phone.

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Applies to PayGuard for Android, version 1.0.0

The short version

  • PayGuard has no account, no login and no server of its own.
  • Your shifts, pay figures, payslip photos, notes and cases are stored only on your device, encrypted.
  • We never receive, see or sell your records, photos or payslips.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • The only network use is the optional Premium subscription, handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • You can erase everything at any time with Delete all data.

1. Who we are

PayGuard (package com.harismughl.payguard) is an offline-first Android app that helps you record shifts, enter payslip figures and compare them. It is published by Haris Mughal (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, PayGuard stores the following on your phone only. It is created by you and never sent to us:

  • Jobs, shifts, pay rates and pay terms you enter.
  • Payslip figures you type in, and optional payslip photos with text read from them by on-device OCR.
  • Comparison results, notes, case timelines and evidence you save.
  • Reminders and app settings.
  • An optional name and postal address, only if you type them (used on the PDF you choose to create).
  • Your trial start date and Premium status, so the app can unlock features.

Because we have no server, we cannot access, recover or restore this data for you.

3. Data we collect

PayGuard itself does not collect personal data. The single exception is the optional Premium subscription, which uses RevenueCat together with Google Play Billing. For that purpose only, the following leaves your device:

DataPurpose
An anonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCatTo recognise your subscription
Purchase and entitlement status, product purchased, purchase token / receiptTo verify and unlock Premium, and to restore it on a new device
Basic device and app information (e.g. app version, OS version, country/currency from the store)Subscription processing and fraud prevention

This traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Your shifts, pay data, payslips and photos are never part of it. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card number.

The 14-day free trial runs on your device and needs no card or account.

4. What PayGuard never asks for

Location, contacts, email address, phone number, bank details, tax ID, social security or national insurance numbers, microphone, SMS or call history. There is no advertising ID, no analytics SDK and no crash-upload service.

5. How your data is protected

  • The database is encrypted (SQLite3 Multiple Ciphers, AES). Payslip photos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Keys are random and are held in the Android Keystore.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app, so the system does not copy your records elsewhere.
  • Optional app lock (fingerprint, face or screen lock) hides PayGuard in recent apps and blocks screenshots. It is a privacy screen on your phone, not an account.
  • Text recognition runs on-device using a bundled model; payslip images are not uploaded.

Encryption protects data at rest. It cannot protect against a phone that is already compromised.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or share your records with anyone. The only third-party services involved are:

  • Google Play / Google Play Billing — processes payments and subscriptions (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — processes iOS payments and subscriptions (Apple Privacy Policy).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (RevenueCat Privacy Policy).
  • Google ML Kit text recognition — bundled in the app and run entirely on your device for reading payslips.

We may disclose information only if required by law, which is unlikely since we hold none of your records.

8. Exports and backups

You can create an encrypted backup file or a PDF personal record. These are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them through the Android file or share dialogs. Backups are protected by a password you choose; it cannot be recovered if you forget it. Once a file leaves the app, its handling is up to you and the app you share it with.

9. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you remove it. Settings → Data & security → Delete all data immediately erases the database, encrypted files, reminders and encryption keys. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, also removes it. Backups or PDFs you saved elsewhere are not touched, so delete those yourself.

Important: deleted or lost data cannot be recovered by us. Make a backup if you want to keep it.

Subscription records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept under their own policies. To ask RevenueCat to delete data tied to your anonymous ID, contact us (section 13).

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because your records exist only on your device, you already have full control: view, edit, export and delete them inside the app. For the limited subscription data, contact us and we will help with your request.

11. Children

PayGuard is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how PayGuard handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date, and the in-app privacy text. Material changes will be highlighted in the app update notes.
