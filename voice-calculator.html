Voice Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Voice Calculator ("the app") is a free calculator that accepts spoken and typed input. This policy explains how the app, Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics handle information.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap the mic button, and only to understand your calculation. The app does not record or store audio.

Speech recognition

Your device's speech service (usually Google) converts speech to text. Audio may be sent to that service during recognition and is subject to that provider's privacy policy. The app itself does not upload audio to our servers, AdMob or Firebase Analytics.

Calculation history & settings

Calculation history and settings are stored on your device. You can delete history from the History screen. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Only calculator settings are included in Android backup; calculation history is excluded.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display bottom banner advertisements. To deliver and measure ads, analyse performance and prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information including:

  • Android Advertising ID and other device or app identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information, such as device type and operating system
  • App and advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Information may be shared with Google and its advertising partners for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by these SDKs is encrypted in transit. Advertising SDK data is separate from your locally stored calculation history and voice input.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents regional privacy messages. When available, Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you review or change your advertising choices. Android's advertising settings also provide controls for your Advertising ID. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily remove advertisements or processing needed for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Usage analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and improve reliability. This can include app opens, visits to fixed screens such as Calculator and Settings, basic app/device information, app-instance identifiers and approximate location derived from network information. We do not send your calculation expressions, results, calculation history, spoken words or audio to Firebase Analytics.

You can turn usage analytics on or off in Settings → Usage analytics. It defaults on only when the consent system reports that consent is not required and you have not saved a preference; otherwise it remains off until you enable it. Firebase advertising consent categories are disabled. Turning off usage analytics stops future Firebase Analytics collection by the app; it does not disable AdMob advertising or delete information previously processed by Google.

Google processes information under its privacy terms. Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Firebase privacy information

Accounts & developer servers

The app does not require an account. We do not operate a server that receives your calculations, history or audio. Google services process the advertising and analytics information described above under their own retention and privacy controls.

Children

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
